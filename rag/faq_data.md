# Health1st Clinic — Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you accept insurance?
We accept most major insurance providers. Please have your insurance card ready when you arrive, and our front desk will verify your coverage before your visit. If you're unsure whether your specific plan is accepted, we recommend calling the clinic directly to confirm before booking.

## What are your clinic hours?
General clinic hours are Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Individual providers may have different hours within that window — for example, Dr. Bilal Khan (Cardiology) sees patients until 2:00 PM, while Dr. Ahmed Raza (General Physician) is available until 5:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays.

## Where is the clinic located?
Health1st Clinic is located in the main medical plaza. Parking is available on-site, free for the first hour and discounted with a validated ticket from the front desk for longer visits.

## Do I need a referral to see a specialist?
For our Cardiologist, a referral from a general physician is recommended but not strictly required for a first visit — we can also arrange an internal referral if you first see our General Physician and they recommend it. For our Pediatrician, no referral is needed.

## What should I bring to my first appointment?
Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of any current medications you're taking, and any relevant prior medical records if you're transferring from another clinic.

## Do I need to fast before my appointment?
This depends on the appointment type. Routine checkups and follow-ups typically don't require fasting. If your visit involves specific bloodwork, our staff will let you know in advance whether fasting is needed — if you're unsure, it's fine to eat normally before a general or follow-up visit.

## What's your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
You can cancel or reschedule an appointment through this phone line at any time, free of charge, as long as it's done before your scheduled time. We appreciate as much advance notice as possible so the slot can be offered to another patient.

## Do you accept walk-ins?
We primarily operate on a scheduled-appointment basis to keep wait times reasonable, but urgent same-day requests are sometimes accommodated depending on provider availability — it's best to call ahead rather than arrive unannounced.

## Do you offer telehealth or video appointments?
Not at this time — all appointments at Health1st Clinic are in-person visits.

## How do I get a prescription refill?
Prescription refills should be requested by calling the clinic directly and speaking with staff, or through a follow-up appointment with your provider. We're not able to authorize refills through this booking line.

## What appointment types do you offer?
We currently offer three appointment types: New Patient Consult (45 minutes, for anyone visiting a provider for the first time), Follow-up (15 minutes, for existing patients continuing care), and Routine Checkup (30 minutes, for general wellness visits).

## Which providers do you have, and what are their specialties?
Dr. Ahmed Raza is our General Physician, Dr. Sana Malik is our Pediatrician, and Dr. Bilal Khan is our Cardiologist. Each provider offers a different subset of appointment types — for example, our Pediatrician sees new patient consults and follow-ups, but not routine checkups.

## Can a family member book an appointment on my behalf?
Yes, someone can call and book on behalf of a family member, as long as they can provide the patient's name and contact information at the time of booking.

## What happens if I'm running late to my appointment?
Please call the clinic as soon as you know you'll be late. Depending on how delayed you are and the provider's schedule, we may be able to accommodate you, or we may need to reschedule to protect other patients' appointment times.

## Is there a fee for cancelling or missing an appointment?
There's no fee for cancelling in advance. Repeated no-shows without cancellation may be noted on your patient record and could affect future same-day booking priority.
